# Walking basics: a happier walk, together

Suggested Academy module ID: `walking-basics` (pending Maria's content schema).
Owner: Carol. Reading time: approximately 2 minutes. No XP amount assumed.

Learning objective: prepare a comfortable walk, allow exploration and recognize
that GPS statistics are estimates rather than an exercise prescription.

The lesson is rendered at `/walks/learn`. Sections: before you step outside;
let your dog explore; know when to head home; one quiet sniffing break to try.

Knowledge check: Does missing GPS mean your dog has stopped moving?
Correct answer: No. Browser suspension or poor signal can interrupt positions.
Feedback: Check the walk yourself; missing positions cannot prove stationarity.
